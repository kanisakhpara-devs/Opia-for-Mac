# Instrument samples

The recordings in this folder come from the **Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont** by Frank Wen, as rendered to
individual notes by Benjamin Gleitzman's [midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) project.

They are used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).
Opia includes every third semitone of each instrument, unmodified, and shifts pitch at playback for the notes in between.

Instruments: acoustic_grand_piano, electric_piano_1, acoustic_guitar_nylon, electric_guitar_clean, electric_bass_finger, string_ensemble_1, violin, cello, flute, trumpet.
